## Understanding the level system to rank up

Levels are how you rank up on survival.rocks. You earn them by doing things you would probably do anyway, like playing, crafting, completing advancements, spending time in voice chat, posting screenshots to Discord, creating warps, and more. The more levels you collect, the more features and commands you unlock. When you gain levels, the server will tell you exactly what you just unlocked. You can find exactly what you unlock at what level on https://survival.rocks/levels.

Every 360 levels you enter a new phase. Your phase shows up in chat as dots before your name, so others can see how far along you are. The color of those dots shifts gradually through a full color wheel as you level up, so no two phases look exactly the same. You can check your current levels, phase, and color at any time with `/levels` in-game.
